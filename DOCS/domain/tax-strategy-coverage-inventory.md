# Tax strategy coverage inventory

**Last source verification: 2026-08-07.**

This inventory sets the strongest claim an implementation cockpit may make from the public
RetireGolden engine today. It is a claim-control document, not a list of everything the tax law
permits. A strategy can appear in a cockpit only to the extent that the shipped model, cited rule
source, and linked tests support the wording in **Allowed cockpit-v1 claim**. “Partial” means the
engine can support a narrower planning claim, not an execution-ready instruction.

All results remain planning-grade estimates. They do not prepare a return, establish eligibility,
select a custodian transaction, or replace review by the client's tax and legal professionals.
Current-year parameters and primary-source links are maintained in the
[domain rules reference](domain-rules-reference.md).

Statutory authority is not restated here. Every rule the engine implements has a record in the typed, frozen rule
registry ([`taxRuleRegistry.ts`](../../packages/engine/src/rules/taxRuleRegistry.ts)) carrying the quoted
authority, the reading taken, the jurisdiction, the verification date, the implementing sources, and a
classification — `settled`, `approximated` with a required `errorDirection`, `outOfScope`, or `unsettled`. Where a
limitation below has a record, this inventory names the record ID rather than repeating the citation: the registry
is the machine-checked chain and this table is the claim boundary. An `approximated` record is the one to read
before broadening a claim, because it means the engine returns a number that is knowably not the authority's and
states which way it errs.

The partial owned-IRA action substrate now treats each per-allocation early-distribution penalty
candidate as provisional. Fully evidenced allocations are aggregated by owner, tax year, and exact
rational rate, rounded once, and allocated by floor quotas plus deterministic largest remainders.
An unresolved same-rate member keeps its whole rate bucket provisional, and any unresolved
allocation still suppresses the entire owner/year annual-finalization bundle. This remains
planning-grade evidence rather than execution readiness.

The actions package also has an isolated provisional validator for one current owned-IRA SEPP
scheduled-payment transition. It derives actual gross and character from canonical coverage, validates
the raw schedule reference against explicit election/opening/history evidence, binds predecessor-after
to current-previous state, and rejects replay found in the supplied complete history. A separate annual
reconciler now reconstructs every current-year history from raw payments, takes coverage only from a
structurally verified complete inventory, requires exact inventory/tuple membership and terminal annual
gross, and rejects lifetime replay and cross-kind ID reuse, forks, duplicates, omissions, foreign members,
and extras. Prior-election history must terminate exactly the day before the current tax year, same-day
identities use locale-independent UTF-16 ordering, and new recursive/annual structural IDs are fixed-width
SHA-256 digests. Legacy empty current-year history remains accepted without explicit terminal proof by
safely inferring the opening; populated legacy history without terminal proof fails closed. Its
completeness is only relative to the explicitly supplied inventory. The penalty prerequisite now accepts
routed raw annual inputs, rederives the complete source inventory from canonical annual character, runs
the reconciler internally, and exact-rejoins complete payments. Only `reconciled` routes can produce final
`iraSeppQualified` zero-penalty decisions; every non-success route stays pending and supplies no
negative-SEPP authority. All-basis members receive coverage but no evaluation, age and disability retain
precedence, and qualified SIMPLE members bypass participation/rate facts. The annual finalizer and
coordinator now forward raw routes, consume complete qualified outcomes, and expose detailed canonical
route diagnostics when blocked. Compact route bindings flow transitively into finalization and candidate
binding IDs while legacy no-route IDs remain unchanged. The public staged-date ID builder only reproduces
candidate-bound planning evidence; exact coordinator rejoin remains authoritative, and no boundary
establishes actionability, movement, execution, or simulation.

The outer Plan-owned annual coordinator now derives the complete owner/year ordinary-withdrawal batch
and owned non-Roth IRA pool from Plan identity, ownership, classification, and request data. It requires
complete opening and Form 8606 year-end snapshots, annual basis/line-7 and line-8 inventories from one
ledger run, plus exact alive evidence. This is Plan-identity-authoritative, runtime-snapshot-bound
planning evidence; it still establishes neither committed movement nor actionability.

The standalone pure executor that committed exact cents from that coordinator input has been removed. The
engine settles owned-IRA basis on the live path instead: `projection/simulate.ts` drives
`internal/ownedNonRothIraAnnualAttemptSettlement.ts` over the generic bounded-attempt driver, settling from
runtime occurrences rather than from a Plan-shaped candidate/execution chain. The coordinator above remains
a pure planning-evidence boundary with no commit arm.
The separate annual physical-event inventory now derives traditional-account Plan allocations and
exact-rejoins a complete runtime inventory for RMD, SEPP, legacy projection, IRA/employer-plan
in-year account-balance contribution inflows, and configured employer match into one immutable
Plan/year chronology. Aggregate legacy QCD reclassification, annuity funding, rollover inflows, and
other traditional transfers remain unresolved until their producer and physical endpoints have a typed
binding contract. Following-year IRA
contributions designated for the prior tax year remain
separate annual-basis facts rather than calendar-year physical events. Shared
movement authority is limited to source members with identical owner/kind/origin/date/sequence bindings;
member upstream evidence and attestation upstream lineage remain explicit. It publishes owned-IRA views
and provisional line-7, line-8, and QCD routing categories, but deliberately changes no balance, basis,
tax, penalty, `YearResult`, or simulator state. Unresolved identity/date/order, omissions, collisions,
and unsafe totals fail closed.

The pure `buildPlanOwnedNonRothIraAnnualPostCandidateClassificationInput` API now exact-rejoins the
standalone inventory, staged candidate, complete owner-wide December 31 pool, basis, and following-year
contribution evidence into a frozen classifier input without classifying, executing, or integrating with
projection. The normative contract is in
[domain rules §16](domain-rules-reference.md#16-account-eligibility-hsa-nondeductible-basis-and-fixed-asset-disposition-opt-in).

Three identity-bearing executors now commit inside the annual projection rather than only in isolated boundaries:
the exact-cent ordinary-withdrawal executor, the named Roth-conversion executor, and the named-QCD executor —
`simulate.ts` calls the QCD execution prerequisite, physical staging and executor, which run the tax-character
post-pass and owner-wide pool capacity behind them. A fourth movement exists only as a pair: a named conversion
whose tax is funded by a named sibling withdrawal executes as an atomic group or is refused, and that arm is
reachable only for an owner whose aggregated nondeductible basis is proven zero. Separately, an aggregate QCD is
no longer conditional on an RMD, and its beyond-RMD debits carry a `legacyQcd` occurrence through the owned-IRA
evidence chain. None of this moves the claim boundary for an aggregate result.

Two boundaries govern how far a named QCD may be described. Its RMD coordination is not modeled — the annual pass
distributes the whole required amount in cash before any gift is sized, so `rmdSatisfiedAmount` is structurally
zero and the record says so in a typed `coordination` field rather than leaving the zero to be read as a fact
(`treas-reg-1-408-8-g-projection-named-qcd-beyond-rmd`, `approximated` / `overstatesTax`, pinned by a `produced`
fixture). And no named QCD produces a charitable deduction: the executor refuses the whole batch whenever any gift
would leave a positive §170 amount.

What remains uncalled by `simulate.ts` is the Plan-owned annual coordinator and its post-candidate evidence
builder, the SEPP validator and annual reconciler, the employer-plan withdrawal-character and penalty boundaries,
and the `annualQcd*` evidence, finalization and §170 deduction stack below the executor; each still reports
`movement: notCommitted`. The standalone Plan-owned commit boundary
that used to head that list has been removed — `simulate.ts` settles owned-IRA basis from runtime
occurrences through `internal/ownedNonRothIraAnnualAttemptSettlement.ts`.

The optimizer's `identityIncomplete` veto on an aggregate conversion schedule is now conditional rather than
absolute. A promotion loop names the owners, sources, destinations and exact cents by the same allocation policy
the ledger executes, prices the named candidate on its own exact-ledger run, and the tournament publishes it only
when the two projections are cent-identical or the named one earns a recommendation on its own metrics. A solver
winner is never promotable. So the strongest conversion claim now depends on which arm produced the schedule, and
the table below states both.

## How cockpit code should use this inventory

1. Preserve the exact wording boundary in the **Allowed cockpit-v1 claim** column or use narrower
   language.
2. Carry the applicable limitation into the evaluation and any handoff artifact; a footnote hidden
   elsewhere is not enough for an action amount.
3. Suppress an action when its prerequisite is open. A modeled annual household amount is not
   evidence for a person, account, custodian, charity, security lot, deadline, or tax form.
4. Keep the calculation result separate from an advisor's later adoption, annotation, and
   professional-confirmation record.
5. Add source and test evidence here before broadening a claim. The absence of a failing test does
   not establish coverage.
