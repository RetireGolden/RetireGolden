## 2. Long-term capital gains and NIIT (2026)

LTCG + qualified dividends stack **on top of** ordinary taxable income:

| Rate | Single (taxable income) | MFJ |
|------|------------------------|-----|
| 0% | ≤ $49,450 | ≤ $98,900 |
| 15% | ≤ $545,500 | ≤ $613,700 |
| 20% | above | above |

- **Preferential rate schedule:** section 1(h)(1) supplies the 0/15/20 percent layers; section 1(j)(5)(B) and Rev. Proc. 2025-32 supply the 2026 maximum zero / 15-percent rate amounts the pack stores (`irc-1-h-1-0-15-20-preferential-rate-schedule`). Ordinary income fills brackets first; gains are taxed by where they land (`irc-1-h-capital-gain-stacked-on-ordinary`). The optimizer linearizes every taxable-bucket gain at a flat 15 percent instead (`irc-1-h-optimizer-flat-fifteen-percent-preferential-rate`, approximated / bothDirections).
- **Capital-loss carryforward:** after the section 1211(b) $3,000 ordinary-income allowance, section 1212(b)(1) carries the unabsorbed pool into succeeding years with no fixed expiration (`irc-1212-b-capital-loss-carryforward`). Section 1212(b)(2) preserves that pool in a zero-income year; the engine still burns up to $3,000 from the pool (`irc-1212-b-2-zero-income-section-1211-allowance-preserves-carryforward`, approximated / overstatesTax).
- **Wash sales:** section 1091(a) disallows a loss when substantially identical stock or securities are acquired in the 61-day window; the engine emits and deducts realized taxable-account losses without that check (`irc-1091-a-wash-sale-thirty-day-window`, approximated / bothDirections). Rev. Rul. 2008-5 permanently disallows the loss (with no IRA basis increase) when the individual's IRA buys the replacement stock — not modelled (`rev-rul-2008-5-ira-wash-sale-permanent-loss-disallowance`, outOfScope).
- **Basis at death:** section 1014(a)(1) generally gives inherited property date-of-death FMV basis. The estate metric treats taxable balances as stepped-up (no heir tax on embedded gain) while the projection never writes FMV onto `costBasis`, so a post-death sale can still tax the pre-death embedded gain (`irc-1014-a-1-basis-at-death-fair-market-value`, approximated / bothDirections).
- **Qualified opportunity funds:** section 1400Z-2 defers eligible gain invested in a QOF and can elect FMV basis after ten years. Pub. L. 119-21 made the program permanent for post-2026 investments (rolling five-year deferral; legacy December 31, 2026 cutoffs still govern pre-2027 elections). Not modelled (`irc-1400z-2-qof-deferral-and-ten-year-basis-election`, outOfScope).
- **NIIT:** 3.8% on net investment income above MAGI $200,000 single / $250,000 MFJ — **never indexed**, so it bites more each year.
