## KS — approve

**Authority and recomputation.** [K.S.A. 79-32,117(c)(xix)](https://ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch79/079_032_0117.html) names benefits under Washburn University's retirement plan. [Kansas Revenue Notice 08-06](https://www.ksrevenue.gov/taxnotices/notice08-06.pdf) identifies its basic plan as a §403(b) defined-contribution plan and allows the subtraction for both that plan and its supplemental retirement annuity, including lump sums. Thus a federally included $12,000 Washburn employer-plan payment coded `KS-WASHBURN` has a **$12,000** subtraction, not zero. An ordinary IRA or an employer row declared `401k`, `401a`, `457b`, or `ira` has no Washburn identity from its plan type; without a code its named-plan subtraction is zero and the year is complete. A taxable `403b`, `other`, `unknown`, or undeclared employer row without a code could be Washburn and is incomplete; with `KS-WASHBURN`, it subtracts the included amount. The distinction follows the named-system statute and notice.

**Match and consistency.** `stateMidwestExtras.ts#kansasNamedPlanExclusion` implements those branches, with the warning tied to the actual registry ID. `stateCluster47.production.rules.test.ts` pins all four no-subtraction types, all four potentially Washburn types with and without code, the $12,000 coded subtraction, and the zero-amount boundary. `simulate.stateWorksheetZeroMateriality.test.ts` pins propagation of a declared `401k`, the missing-code warning for `403b` and undeclared plans, and the coded Washburn taxable-income decrease. The Kansas rule record and `DOCS/domain/state-tax-research/KS.md` now name the Washburn exception and explicitly leave employer-entered federal Thrift Savings Plan and KPERS 457 treatment unresolved; they no longer claim that all employer plans are ineligible. **Verdict: approve.**

## Side effect — reject

**Rule and authority.** `stateTax.ts` feeds `stateWestExtras.ts#virginiaPriorStateBasisSubtraction` and `pensionBasisPools` using record `va-code-58-1-322-02-11-basis`. [Va. Code §58.1-322.02(11)](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title58.1/chapter3/section58.1-322.02/) enumerates qualified §401 pension/profit-sharing/stock-bonus plans, §408 IRAs/annuities, §457 deferred-compensation plans, and federal-government retirement programs, subject to federally included income and contributions taxed by another state. A genuine 401(k) is under §401 and a 457(b) is under §457, so declaring either can establish the enumerated *plan class*; prior-state tax and remaining basis still must be proved. A §403(b) plan is a distinct statutory class, not §401, §408, or §457 merely because it is an employer retirement account. The [IRS governmental-plan classification](https://www.irs.gov/government-entities/federal-state-local-governments/government-retirement-plans-toolkit) separately lists §401(a), §403(b), and §457(b). A federal-government-program exception would require separate evidence; the `403b` label alone does not establish it.

**Effect and match.** `annualStateRetirementEvents.ts#stateRetirementEventsFromAccountAmounts` now copies a traditional employer account's declared `employerPlanType` into a row lacking `qualifiedPlanType`. Virginia's `enumerated` predicate accepts *every* employer-plan type other than `other` and `unknown`, so a declared `403b` with, for example, $4,000 federally included income, $6,000 asserted previously taxed basis, and a non-VA `priorTaxState` wrongly subtracts $4,000 and consumes that basis. The existing Virginia basis fixture tests `401a` but has no `403b` negative case. This is a real reachable change because event evidence can supply `priorTaxState` and `knownPreviouslyTaxedBasis`.

**Other readers.** Grepping production reads of `qualifiedPlanType` found Kansas's named-plan leaf (the intended behavior reviewed above); Utah's prior-taxed-§401(a) leaf and `pensionBasisPools` (they require exactly `401a`, which an account's declared `employerPlanType` enum cannot supply, so no new Utah eligibility); and Massachusetts/Virginia basis-ledger key fallbacks. This event path always has `accountId`, so its newly copied type does not alter the Massachusetts key. `mapStateIncomeComponent` and the annuity path already derive types independently. No other state's monetary rule gains eligibility from this mapping. The Virginia basis record's enumeration is correct; the predicate is broader than that record. **Verdict: reject.** Exclude `403b` from Virginia's employer-plan predicate and its basis-pool predicate, and pin the negative case while retaining the `401k` and `457b` positives.
